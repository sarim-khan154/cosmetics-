## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial luxury ethos for high-end skincare, cosmetics, and botanical beauty. It caters to a discerning, modern, and diverse audience seeking intentional, sensorially rich rituals over standard transactional e-commerce.

The visual direction fuses **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Luminous Tactility**:
- **Generous Spatial Rhythm**: Broad margins and breathable layouts mirror luxury print editorials and high-fashion catalogs.
- **Sensorial Depth**: Diffused ambient glows, subtle glassmorphic scrims, and soft gradients evoke radiance, dewy textures, and inner illumination.
- **Warm Contrast**: Deep, nuanced charcoal-espresso typography balances soft botanical blush and warm nude surfaces, delivering accessible legibility without the harshness of pitch black.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by soft, biological skin tones and radiant metallics:

- **Primary (`#211C1D`)**: Deep charcoal-espresso used for display titles, foundational copy, high-priority actions, and structured borders.
- **Secondary (`#C9937B`)**: Warm rose-gold accent. Applied to active states, bespoke badges, highlighted attributes, and radiant focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373`)**: Muted champagne gold. Used for subtle iconography, ratings, luxury accolades, and secondary hover highlights.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FAF7F5`)**: Luminous ivory white that serves as the base viewport background, keeping the canvas warm and tactile rather than sterile.
- **Surface Containers**:
  - `surface-container-low`: `#FCEEE9` (Soft blush pink) for subtle card surfaces and ambient zones.
  - `surface-container`: `#F7E1D7` (Warm botanical nude) for hover treatments, inset blocks, and chips.
  - `surface-container-high`: `#EED9C4` (Warm nude beige) for structural dividers and contrast tiers.

### Interaction & System Accents
- **Success / Botanical Green**: `#4E6548` (muted eucalyptus) for clean ingredient certifications and verification.
- **Warning / Alert**: `#A35C52` (soft terracotta) for stock limits and form validation warnings.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-fashion editorial styling with technical clarity:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical refinement and heritage. Used for hero phrases, editorial section intros, product collection titles, and sensory quotes. Display titles favor lighter weights (`400` or `500`) with tight negative letter spacing for an elevated silhouette.
- **Body & Technical Specs (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric balance, human warmth, and exceptional legibility across dense ingredient listings, clinical trial results, and long-form beauty guides.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) on small scales to organize collection categories, filter groups, and botanical potency markers with precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model prioritizes expansive negative space and graceful responsive adaptation:

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` canvas margins, and `1.5rem` gutters. Editorial hero modules and collection showrooms can collapse to asymmetric 6/6 or 8/4 splits to emphasize imagery.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Single-column card stacks transition to horizontal carousels with peek overflow for shade swatches and formula sets.
- **Rhythm & White Space**: Internal card padding uses generous `space-lg` to `space-xl` values, preventing the content density from degrading the luxury aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on luminous luminescence and soft tonal boundaries rather than aggressive drop shadows:

- **Ambient Luminescence (Glows)**:
  - Surfaces utilize wide-spread, low-opacity warm gaussian glows: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(201, 147, 123, 0.12)`.
  - Focused interactive elements (e.g., active shade picker, floating ritual tray) radiate a subtle inner bloom: `0 0 24px rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.25)`.
- **Luminous Glassmorphism**:
  - Navigation bars, sticky purchase bars, and shade filtering drawers use translucent backing (`rgba(250, 247, 245, 0.82)`) with a `16px` to `20px` background blur and a delicate hairline outline (`rgba(201, 147, 123, 0.18)`).
- **Hairline Framing**:
  - Structural separators, cards, and input fields utilize delicate 1px boundaries in `#EED9C4` or low-opacity rose gold (`rgba(201, 147, 123, 0.25)`), preserving clean definition across soft blush backdrops.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, pill-shaped aesthetic that reflects smooth cosmetic stones, drop applicators, and botanical contours:

- **Primary Controls & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` or 9999px border-radius) for all call-to-action buttons, tag filters, and search pills.
- **Surfaces & Cards**: Medium surfaces scale with `rounded-xl` (1.5rem to 2rem radius), creating a soft, tactile cushion aesthetic.
- **Media Containers**: Product galleries, sensory video panels, and model swatches feature arched or soft rounded-2xl headers, echoing high-end boutique mirrors and classic architectural curves.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid deep charcoal-espresso (`#211C1D`) background with ivory white (`#FAF7F5`) typography, full pill curvature, uppercase tracking (`0.08em`), and a warm rose-gold glow on hover (`rgba(201, 147, 123, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Botanical Outline**: Luminous ivory base, 1px `#C9937B` border, charcoal text. Transitions on hover to `#FCEEE9` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Glass Floating Action**: Frosted glass surface, blurred backdrop, framed in a fine 1px champagne gold border.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Card**: Set on `#FCEEE9` or white canvas with a 1px `#EED9C4` border, `rounded-xl` geometry, generous internal padding (`space-lg`), and subtle image zoom transitions.
- **Shade Swatches**: Small circular swatches displaying true cosmetic shades, encircled by a 1px transparent track that shifts to a solid rose-gold `#C9937B` halo when selected.

### Chips & Filters
- **Botanical Tag / Filter Pill**: Compact height (32px), pill-shaped, `#FAF7F5` background with hairline `#EED9C4` border. Active state shifts to `#211C1D` with white text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: Minimalist pill-rounded or softened floating fields with `#FAF7F5` fill and a 1px `#EED9C4` border. Focus state activates a subtle 1px `#C9937B` border and a delicate `0 0 12px rgba(201, 147, 123, 0.15)` glow.
- **Quantity & Dosage Selectors**: Pill-shaped capsule unit housing decrement, value, and increment controls with centered typographic alignment.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls**: Circular selectors across both radios and checkboxes to maintain organic cohesion. Checked state displays a solid `#211C1D` disc centered within a rose-gold rim.

### Ritual / Ingredient Accordion
- **Collapsible Drawers**: Borderless stacked rows partitioned by 1px `#EED9C4` horizontal rules, featuring fluid expand/collapse transitions, serif category titles, and refined sans-serif clinical details.
