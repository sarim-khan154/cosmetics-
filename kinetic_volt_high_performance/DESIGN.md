## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges elite track-and-field precision engineering with raw, high-tempo modern streetwear energy. It projects velocity, structural discipline, uncompromising agility, and an electric edge. The target audience includes performance-obsessed athletes, forward-thinking sneaker purists, and urban runners who demand footwear that transitions from biomechanical excellence to gallery-level street presence.

The visual execution operates within **High-Contrast Precision Brutalism mixed with Technical Kinetic Minimalism**:
- **Massive condensed typography** commands primary hero layers and dynamic specs.
- **Architectural micro-borders** (1px crisp structural frames) provide industrial, wind-tunnel containment.
- **Aggressive contrast** sets deep obsidian foundations directly against stark optic whites and razor-sharp volt neon shocks.
- Visual rhythm is hyper-intentional: generous negative space around hero products paired with densely structured mechanical telemetry (drop specs, cushioning grams, foam responsiveness tags).

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark-mode velocity, high nocturnal contrast, and luminescent sporting alerts.

- **Volt Electric Lime (`#D2F800`)**: The primary kinetic catalyst. Reserved for active states, key conversion triggers (Primary Add to Bag, Checkout, Reserve Drop), speed badges, and active telemetry indicators. It must never be diluted by soft pastels or muddy gradients.
- **Infrared Alert (`#FF4800`)**: The secondary kinetic accelerator. Used for high-urgency notifications, limited-edition release drops, race-day warnings, and micro-accents alongside the Volt highlight.
- **Optic White (`#FFFFFF`)**: The tertiary structural tone. Dictates condensed display headlines, legible technical labels, and high-priority interactive icons.
- **Deep Obsidian (`#0B0B0C`)**: The grounding void. Represents the primary background canvas. Paired with tonal sub-surfaces (`#141416` for elevated containers, `#1C1C1F` for active card borders and interactive surfaces).
- **Mist & Steel Gray (`#27272A` to `#71717A`)**: Governs secondary metrics, biomechanical specs, disabled states, and hair-thin linear architecture.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate velocity hierarchy:
- **Headlines (`Anton`)**: Always set in full uppercase. Condensed, massive, and unapologetic. Designed to emulate track-side timing boards, high-speed stadium graphics, and technical athletic packaging. Kerning is slightly opened on display headers (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to preserve legibility at breakneck scales.
- **Interface & Technical Body (`Inter`)**: Neutral, cold, and mathematically balanced to ground the raw impact of Anton. Set in sentence case for reading comfort and strict tabular uppercase for SKU identifiers, foam densities, milligram weights, and drop dimensions.
- **Labels & Spec Badges**: Formatted in bold uppercase with expanded tracking (`+0.08em` to `+0.12em`) to mimic aerospace laser-etched markings and product prototypes.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an industrial fluid grid designed to display footwear assets with minimal UI friction:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `3rem` margins. High-speed split layouts allow oversized footwear visuals to dominate 7-8 columns while technical specs, sizing grids, and checkout controls stay anchored in a 4-5 column cockpit.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Product lists default to high-density 2-up cards.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` edge margins. Primary interaction shifts cleanly to a fixed bottom dock and fluid swipe carousels.

Spacing rhythm is disciplined around standard base increments. Product hero showcases maintain wide breathing room (`space-xl`), while micro-specs, sizing selectors, and product detail strips lock tightly (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to project mechanical rigor.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating diffused dropshadows. Instead, elevation and physical presence are articulated through **tonal stratification and razor-sharp linear micro-borders**:

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas)**: Pure deep obsidian (`#0B0B0C`).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Product Tiles)**: Carbon surface (`#141416`) rimmed with a 1px crisp outline (`#27272A`).
- **Layer 2 (Hover & Activated Panels)**: Kinetic surface (`#1C1C1F`) framed by a 1px border shifting dynamically to Volt Lime (`#D2F800`) or Infrared (`#FF4800`).
- **Layer 3 (Overlays & Sticky Mobile Navigation)**: Glass-composite obsidian (`rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.88)`) with `16px` backdrop-blur and a 1px optic white top hairline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Active Glows**: When extreme kinetic focus is demanded (e.g., active drop status or cart notification), use a tight 0px-blur 1px outer ring in `#D2F800` paired with an optional 8px ambient aura at 20% opacity.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is **strictly sharp (`0px` corner radius)** across the entirety of the UI ecosystem. 

- Sharp geometry reflects cutting-edge composite carbon plates, aerodynamic wind-shear profiles, and brutalist performance design.
- Buttons, input modules, badge chips, dialogs, sizing blocks, and bottom bars share 90-degree corner precision.
- Subtle 45-degree corner chamfers (dog-eared industrial cuts) may be applied selectively to limited-release countdown badges, prototype release cards, and digital tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic**: Solid Volt Electric Lime (`#D2F800`) fill, pure black (`#0B0B0C`) text in Anton uppercase or heavy Inter, 0px border radius, high-density padding (16px vertical, 28px horizontal). On hover/active: immediate shift to crisp Optic White (`#FFFFFF`) with zero transition delay, signaling instantaneous biomechanical response.
- **Secondary Technical**: Transparent background with a 1px Optic White border (`#FFFFFF`) and white text. On hover: inverts to full white fill with black text.
- **Destructive / Drop Alert**: Solid Infrared (`#FF4800`) with black text for immediate urgency.

### Chips & Performance Badges
- Micro-dimension tags (e.g., `PROPULSION: CARBON`, `WEIGHT: 184G`, `CUSHION: 10MM DROP`).
- Dark slate fill (`#18181B`) with 1px border (`#27272A`), uppercase monospace tracking (`label-caps-mono`), and an inline accent dot (Volt or Infrared).

### Footwear Product Cards
- Sharp rectangular enclosures with zero radius.
- Deep obsidian backdrop (`#141416`) allowing crisp silhouette cutouts of athletic footwear to extend slightly beyond standard visual baselines.
- Real-time stock status badge pinned top-right; price and technical specs pinned flush bottom inside an architectural micro-border grid cell.

### Input Fields & Size Matrices
- **Input Fields**: Flat obsidian containers (`#141416`) with a 1px neutral border (`#3F3F46`). Floating or top-stacked technical labels in uppercase. Focus state switches border instantly to 1px Volt Lime (`#D2F800`).
- **Shoe Size Matrix**: Dense grid of sharp square tiles. Default: transparent with `#27272A` borders. Sold out: struck through with an angled micro-line. Selected: solid Volt Lime (`#D2F800`) with obsidian black text.

### Mobile Bottom Navigation
- Fixed, touch-optimized lower console (56px-64px height) with 88% obsidian blur backdrop and a crisp 1px top stroke.
- High-hit-area 4-tab bar (Home / Explore / Vault Drops / Bag) utilizing minimalist geometric line iconography paired with micro condensed type.
- Active tab illuminated in Volt Lime (`#D2F800`).
